# Separate the no-LLM runtime baseline from the AI-assisted research plane

The baseline system's inference chain excludes LLMs, generative AI and Agents so its capability ceiling can be measured independently. AI/Agents remain allowed and important for research, experiments, synthetic surface generation, candidate annotation and QA under provenance and human-reference gates. This avoids both prematurely embedding AI in the target architecture and unnecessarily banning it from experimental work.
